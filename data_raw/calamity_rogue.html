      Rogue weapons

      From Calamity Mod Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Rogue weapons are weapons that deal rogue damage, and are able to consume stealth when used, which adds a new attack. They belong to the rogue class, a new class added by the Calamity Mod. Rogue weapons are categorized into bombs, boomerangs, daggers, javelins, and spiky balls. There are several other rogue weapons that do not fit into any of these types.

      For an estimate of progression order, sort the table by rarity. For recommendations of items to try at specific tiers of progression, consult the class setups guide.

      Trivia

      • Rogue weapons were previously throwing weapons that dealt throwing-type damage. However, all of the Calamity Mod's throwing weapons were given the rogue damage type in the 1.4.1.001 update. This was partially done in preparation for the throwing class' removal in Terraria 1.4: Journey's End.
