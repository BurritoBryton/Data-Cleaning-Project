      Classless weapons

      From Calamity Mod Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search

      Classless weapons are weapons that belong to no particular class. They can have one of the following damage types: classless damage, which scales with the average of all other classes' damage bonuses; or typeless damage, which scales with the highest class' damage. Classless weapons cannot receive modifiers. They also do not trigger the summon damage penalty.

      For an estimate of progression order, sort the table by rarity. For recommendations of items to try at specific tiers of progression, consult the class setups guide.
