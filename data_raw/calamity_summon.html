      Summon weapons

      From Calamity Mod Wiki
      Jump to navigation Jump to search
      Not to be confused with summoning items for bosses or events.

      Summon weapons are weapons that deal summon damage through minions, sentries, or special weapons that do not require minion or sentry slots. The Calamity Mod does not currently add any new whips, but it changes whip tag damage from additive to multiplicative. Additionally, Calamity adds a mechanic known as the summon damage penalty, which reduces summon damage to 0.75x of its usual value if the player is holding a weapon belonging to a different class.

      For an estimate of progression order, sort the table by rarity. For recommendations of items to try at specific tiers of progression, consult the class setups guide.
